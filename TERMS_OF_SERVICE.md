## 1. What the service is

TrueMile EV is an electric-vehicle telemetry and cost-tracking app. It logs your trips and
charging sessions (via GPS and, optionally, an OBD adapter you connect to your vehicle),
computes analytics such as cost per mile, efficiency, and battery health over time, and backs
your data up to a private cloud account so it survives phone changes and reinstalls.
